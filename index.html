What the Lab Offers

Three ways students engage with real markets

Bloomberg Terminal Access

Hands-on time on a professional-grade terminal — the same tool used across trading floors and research desks.

Real-Time Market Data

Live pricing across equities, fixed income, and FX feeds classroom exercises and independent research.

Virtual Trading Accounts

Demo brokerage accounts let students execute and track trades without risking real capital.
